Terms for Service

We're excited that you're considering Savor by Steph for your next meal or event.

A few good-to-know policies before you order:

  • Large catering orders require at least two weeks lead time. Emergency bulk orders may be accepted depending on availability.
  • A 50% deposit is required to secure your order. Orders are confirmed once payment is received.
  • Cancellation fees apply if orders are cancelled 72 hours or less before the delivery date.
  • You're welcome to pick up your order yourself or send a rideshare driver. Personal delivery is available — fees depend on distance.
  • Cold-pressed juices are made upon request.
  • We accept Zelle at no extra cost, or Cash App with a 1.5% processing fee.
  • Currently serving Charlotte, Concord, Pineville, Huntersville, and Mooresville in NC, plus Fort Mill, Tega Cay, and Rock Hill in SC. We can also cater for events anywhere within the US.
  • Plates are available upon request/availability.
